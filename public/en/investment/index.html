Investment partnership

Core Material JSC welcomes discussions with partners interested in the construction-materials supply chain in Southern Vietnam.

We are studying partnership options for sand sources and dry-sand processing, as well as other construction-material projects. Investment options are assessed against market demand, returns and actual conditions, and are only implemented after Board approval.

Partners we would like to talk to

  • Owners or operators of licensed sand sources, yards and transport
  • Financial partners interested in construction materials
  • Plants with toll-processing and packing capacity
Details of partnership options are shared directly after both parties sign a non-disclosure agreement.
