About Core Material JSC

Connecting raw materials, equipment and technology solutions for the construction-materials industry.

Core Material JSC (Core Material Science And Technology Joint Stock Company) focuses on trading raw materials, materials and equipment for the construction-materials industry. We work with a network of suppliers and technical partners to help customers choose the right materials and equipment for production and construction.

Business areas

Three directions

CORE TRADING

Raw-material trading

Dry sand, white cement, construction raw materials and selected chemicals — with consistent quality and lot traceability.

CORE TECHNOLOGY

Equipment & technology solutions

GRC/GFRC spraying equipment, mortar spraying machines, material-processing equipment and automation — delivered with suppliers and technical partners.

CORE MATERIALS · ROADMAP

Core-branded products

Gradually developing Core-branded products such as graded sand and decorative materials, with clear quality standards and lot traceability.

Core values

INTEGRITY · INNOVATION · PARTNERSHIP
