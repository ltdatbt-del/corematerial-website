GRC / GFRC spraying equipment

Spray systems for glass-fibre reinforced concrete: façade panels, cornices and architectural elements.

Precast production

GRC/GFRC (glass-fibre reinforced concrete) is used to make thin, light yet durable elements: façade cladding, cornices, louvres, planters and decorative architectural pieces.

GRC spraying equipment delivers the mortar and glass fibre onto the mould, giving more uniform output and higher productivity than manual work.

Note: Unlike wall-plastering machines (used on site), GRC spraying equipment serves precast production in the workshop, together with moulds and its own curing process.

Applications

  • Façade panels, cornices, columns, decorative louvres
  • Architectural elements and decorative GFRC products
  • Precast element workshops

System configuration (by supplier)

  • Sprayer and mortar pump
  • Spray gun assembly
  • Auxiliaries: mixing, feeding, compressed air
  • Spare parts
