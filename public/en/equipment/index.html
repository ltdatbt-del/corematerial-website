Precast production

GRC / GFRC spraying equipment

On-site construction

Wall plastering (mortar spraying) machine

Mechanises feeding, pumping and spraying of plaster mortar, reducing manual labour and speeding up construction.

Learn more
Factory automation

Bag handling & palletizing robot

Automated bag handling and palletizing in the factory: less reliance on manual labour, steadier production rhythm.

Learn more
GRC spraying vs. wall plastering? GRC/GFRC spraying equipment is used to produce glass-fibre reinforced concrete elements in the workshop. Wall plastering machines are used on site to mechanise plastering.
