Bag handling & palletizing robot

Bag handling & palletizing robot

Automated bag handling and palletizing in the factory: less reliance on manual labour, steadier production rhythm.

Factory automation

In dry-mortar, cement and stone-powder plants, bag handling and palletizing require a lot of heavy labour. Palletizing robots and automatic feeding systems reduce that effort and stabilise output.

Each project needs a survey of layout, bag type and production rate to select the right configuration.

Survey information

  • Product handled: bag type, bag weight, bag material
  • Payload and target output (bags/hour)
  • Suitable gripper type
  • Conveyors and pallet positions
  • Layout and area safety
