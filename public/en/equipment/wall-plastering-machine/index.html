Wall plastering (mortar spraying) machine

Wall plastering (mortar spraying) machine

Mechanises feeding, pumping and spraying of plaster mortar, reducing manual labour and speeding up construction.

On-site construction

A mortar spraying machine mechanises mortar feeding, pumping and spraying onto walls instead of hand plastering. The mortar is applied evenly, the coat is denser and progress improves.

It works best together with a suitable ready-mixed mortar, a trained crew and a proper operating and cleaning routine.

Suitable mortars

  • Cement-based ready-mixed plaster
  • Other mortars and putties as recommended by the machine maker

Machine configuration

  • Machine body, hopper and mortar pump
  • Mortar hose and spray gun
  • Air compressor (depending on configuration)

Power / water / air

  • Power supply as per machine specification
  • Clean water for mixing and cleaning
  • Compressed air for the spray gun

Operation & cleaning

  • Check and lubricate before each shift
  • Pre-wet hopper, pump and hoses before feeding mortar
  • Flush with clean water after work to prevent blockages
