Wall plastering (mortar spraying) machine

Wall plastering (mortar spraying) machine

Two mortar-spraying machine lines for on-site work: squeeze-type mortar pumps (3 models, long-distance and high-rise pumping) and a mobile screw-type plastering machine.

On-site construction

Shortage of skilled plasterers, rising labour costs and uneven hand-applied quality (hollows, cracks, varying coat thickness) are the bottlenecks of traditional plastering. A mortar pump mechanises the whole chain: mixing – pumping through the line – spraying directly onto the surface.

The machines Core Material offers use the squeeze-pump principle: rollers compress a squeeze hose to push the mortar, so the mortar only travels inside the hose – fewer wearing parts in contact with mortar and quick cleaning.

It works best together with a suitable ready-mixed mortar, a trained crew and a proper operating and cleaning routine.

Applications

  • Spray plastering of walls and ceilings – interior and exterior
  • Grouting the core of lightweight partition walls
  • Spraying mortar onto stay-in-place formwork (expanded metal mesh)
  • Slope, riverbank and embankment protection

Standard set (per manufacturer's quotation)

  • Squeeze pump with 2 squeeze hoses
  • Mortar mixer and anti-settling hopper agitator
  • About 60 m of steel delivery pipe (TW-60: about 30 m) with 45°/90° elbows and stainless clamps
  • 2 × 10 m flexible hoses with camlock couplings
  • Spray gun
  • Exact items and quantities confirmed per order

4-step workflow

  • Site preparation: position the machine and centralise power, water and materials
  • Metered feeding: batch mortar and water by ratio for a consistent mix
  • Pumping & spraying: mortar runs through steel lines and hoses and is sprayed with a dedicated gun
  • Fast cleanout: dedicated sponge balls are circulated through the line – routine cleaning in about 10 minutes (per manufacturer)

Reference productivity (per manufacturer)

  • Traditional hand plastering: about 20 m² per man-day
  • Mixer + hand plastering: about 90 m² per man-day
  • Squeeze-pump spraying: about 200 m² per man-day
  • Actual output depends on mix design, aggregate, admixtures, coat thickness and line layout

Power / water

  • Pump and hopper agitator: 3-phase 220 V / 50 Hz (standard configuration); mixer: single-phase 220 V / 50 Hz
  • Sites on a 3-phase 380 V grid: power arrangement confirmed when ordering
  • Clean water for mixing and cleaning

Operation & cleaning

  • Check the machine and squeeze hose before each shift
  • Pre-wet hopper, squeeze hose and line before feeding mortar
  • Clean the line right after work to prevent blockages
